    THE QUICK START

    Find your word flow.

    1. Pick your pace. Classic and Daily give you five minutes. Sprint gives you 90 seconds. Daily uses the same UTC-date tile sequence for everyone.
    2. Build across five lanes. Each starts with a consonant. The queue shows your next five tiles, but only the front tile can be played or discarded. Tap a lane or press 1–5 to append the queue letter. You can also drag the selected tile onto a lane. After each placement, all lanes have a one-second placement lock.
    3. Keep or skip. Discard the queue with D. Tap the newest discard to use it instead; tap the queue to switch back. Only the top discard can be reused.
    4. Make it count. Submit an English word of 4–10 letters with its Bank button. Each word can score once per round. The lane restarts with the top discard, if any.
    5. Two wildcards, your choice. Choose A–Z, select Use blank, then tap a lane. Blanks don’t consume your queue and score no rare-letter bonus.
    6. Chase a combo. Bank consecutive words within 10 seconds for +1, +2, then +3 bonus points. A rejected word resets the chain.
    1 point per word + 1 per letter beyond four + 3 per gold letter (J K Q V X Z) + combo bonus. Each remaining discard costs 1 point. Bank five words for a one-time +5 milestone bonus.

    Rounds end when the clock expires or you choose Finish. An empty bag still lets you bank words, reuse discards, and spend blanks. Dictionary checks require an internet connection. Scores are stored locally; challenge links share a tile sequence, not a live leaderboard.
