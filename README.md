# PentaLingo

Five word lanes. A ticking clock. Make every letter count!

Build words, chase your best score, and challenge a friend to beat you with the same letters.

## Play now

**[Play PentaLingo](https://jacksonj-kc.github.io/PentaLingo/)**

Play right in your browser on your phone, tablet, or computer. No download or account needed. Keep an internet connection so the game can check your words.

## Choose your challenge

| Mode | Time | The challenge |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Classic** | 5 minutes | Take your time to build clever words and aim for a personal best. |
| **Daily** | 5 minutes | Everyone gets the same letters for the day. Compare scores with friends! |
| **Sprint** | 90 seconds | Think fast and keep the words coming. |

The Daily challenge changes at midnight UTC, which may be a different time where you live.

## How to play

1. **Press Start round.** Your five lanes each begin with a letter. The next letter to use appears in the queue above them.
2. **Build a word.** Tap or click a lane to add the queue letter to its end. On a computer, you can also drag the letter onto a lane. Words must be **4–10 letters long**.
3. **Skip a letter if you need to.** Press **Discard queue** to set it aside and get the next letter. To reuse a discard, tap the newest letter in the discard stack, then tap a lane. Only the newest discard is available. Tap the queue letter to switch back.
4. **Bank your word.** When a lane spells an English word, press its **Bank** button to collect your points. You can score each word only once per round. The lane clears so you can build another word, starting with the newest discard if there is one.
5. **Use a wildcard when you’re stuck.** You have **two blanks per round**. Choose a letter, press **Use blank**, then tap a lane. Your queue letter stays available for your next move.
6. **Keep going until time runs out.** You can also press **Finish round** whenever you’re ready to see your final score. If the bag runs out of letters, you can still bank words, reuse discards, and spend your blanks.

**Playing with a keyboard?** Press **1–5** to place your selected letter in that lane, or **D** to discard the queue letter.

The clock keeps running while you read the instructions or switch away from the game. If a word check cannot connect, your letters stay in place so you can try again.

## Score more points

| What you do | Points |
| --- | --- |
| Bank a word | **+1** |
| Add letters beyond the first four | **+1 per extra letter** |
| Use a gold letter: J, K, Q, V, X, or Z | **+3 per gold letter** |
| Bank words quickly for a combo | **+1 to +3 per word** |
| Bank your fifth word | **+5 once per round** |
| Leave letters in the discard stack at the end | **−1 per letter** |

For example, **CATS** earns **1 point**, **PLANETS** earns **4 points**, and **QUIZ** earns **7 points**, before any combo bonus. A blank used as a gold letter does not earn the gold-letter bonus.

**Build a combo:** Bank your next word within 10 seconds of the last one. Your second quick word earns +1 extra point, your third earns +2, and each further word in the chain earns +3. If a word is rejected or the 10 seconds run out, start a new chain.

Only banked words earn points. Leftover discards can bring your final score below zero, so reuse them when you can!

## Challenge a friend

1. Start a round and press **Copy challenge link**.
2. Send the link to a friend. They can start when they’re ready, with the same letters and time limit.
3. When you finish, press **Copy result** to share your score and challenge link.

You can also enter a friend’s round code in the **Round code** box and press **Load challenge**, then **Start round**.

Playing again with a loaded challenge repeats the same letters. To get a fresh round, choose a game mode after finishing.

Compare your results with each other—the game does not have a live multiplayer scoreboard.

## Beat your personal best

**Your best rounds** saves your highest scores for each mode on the browser and device you’re using. **Word collection** shows the words you’ve banked in your current round.

Your scores won’t follow you to another device or browser. Clearing your browser’s saved data removes your score history.

## Playing on your phone

Tap a lane to place a letter—there’s no need to drag. Each lane has its own large **Bank** button below the letters.

During a round, your score, timer, and queue stay at the top while you scroll through the lanes. Turn your phone sideways if you prefer a wider view; in landscape, the panel scrolls with the board to leave more space for play.

You can still zoom in using your browser. Challenges, scores, and your word collection are below the game board.

## Feel the streak

Letters bounce into place, gold letters sparkle when drawn, and banked words fly toward your score. The reward card celebrates your word and grows more colorful as you build a combo. Its thin green bar shows how long you have to bank the next word in your chain.

Fill the five little stars by banking five words to collect your milestone bonus. At the end, your round summary highlights your longest word, best combo, and a new personal best when you beat your saved score after penalties.

Prefer a quieter screen? Switch **Effects full** to **Effects calm**. You still see your rewards and progress, with the movement switched off. The game also follows your device’s reduced-motion setting.

## Make yourself comfortable

- **Sound** turns game sound effects on or off.
- **Music** turns background music on or off, independently of sound effects.
- Both start off for your first visit. The game remembers your choices when your browser allows it. Music may wait for your first tap or click before starting.
- Tap controls work on phones and tablets. Keyboard controls and visible focus highlights help you move around on a computer.
- Open **What’s left in the bag?** to plan your next move.
